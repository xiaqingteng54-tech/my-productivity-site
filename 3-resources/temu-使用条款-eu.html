6.2 Main Input Signals for Recommender Systems.

Input signals refer to factors that Temu’s recommender systems consider in recommending content. Temu’s recommender systems utilise three categories of main input signals:

(1) user interactions, including (a) your browsing history, products clicked and related time spent, products added to shopping cart, and products purchased (for both product and promotion recommendations); (b) your searching history, products saved to wish list, and your return and refund history (for just product recommendations); and (c) promotional activities previously recommended to you, promotional activities you viewed, clicked, and/or interacted with, and incentives you have received or used (for just promotion recommendations);

(2) item attributes, which vary depending on the type of items being recommended and are explained in further detail in Sections 6.3, 6.5 and 6.6 below; and

(3) contextual information, including your country/region, language, currency settings, device information, and timing of your interactions with the Services.

Temu’s product and promotion recommendations are by default personalised and take into account all three categories of input signals. Temu’s review recommendations are non-personalised and do not consider input signals from your interactions with the Services.

Input signals from user interactions are relatively important because these signals help Temu understand your interests and preferences. Item attributes are relatively important because they help us match products and promotions with your interests and preferences. Item attributes also allow us to prioritise high-quality and popular content. Contextual information is relatively important because it helps us fine tune recommendations in real time to your browsing environment.

While the relative importance of the input signals is subject to change, those related to user interactions are generally considered most important because they are instrumental to Temu’s provision of personalised recommendations tailored to your interests and preferences.

6.3 Personalised Product Recommendations.

Temu’s personalised product recommendations consider various input signals to predict how likely you are to click on or purchase the products we recommend and rank them accordingly. In addition to considering input signals from user interactions and contextual information listed in Section 6.2, Temu’s product recommendations also consider the following input signals related to product attributes:

(1) product intrinsic characteristics (e.g., size, material, and specifications);

(2) product features (e.g., product category, title, price, images, and affiliated store);

(3) product popularity and statistical metrics (e.g., sales volume, average product ratings, how often a product has been shown, clicked, added to shopping cart, added to wish list, purchased, or returned in aggregate terms, and aggregated engagement metrics (e.g., click-through rates and conversion rates));

(4) the relevance of intrinsic product characteristics and product features to your search query for search-based product recommendations; and

(5) whether and how much third-party merchandise partners have paid to promote the products for advertised product recommendations.

6.4 Filtering and Sorting Product Recommendations.

To help you browse product recommendations more efficiently under specific product categories or through your search results, Temu provides the following filtering and sorting options:

(1) filtering optionsdisplayed at the top of each product recommendation listallow you to narrow recommendation results by:

  • product features (e.g., category, colour, size, material, brand, seasonality, and specifications);

  • price range;

  • review rating range; and

  • shipping location.

  • relevance (default order): products most relevant to your interests will be shown first;

  • top sales: the best-selling products will be shown first;

  • most recent: products most recently added on Temu will be shown first;

  • price low to high: products will be displayed from the most affordable one to the most expensive one; and

  • price high to low: products will be displayed from the most expensive one to the most affordable one.

6.5 Personalised Promotion Recommendations.

Temu’s personalised promotion recommendations predict which promotional activities and incentives you may like and rank them accordingly. Promotional activity recommendations sort promotional activities in an order that is most likely to interest you. Once you participate in a promotional activity, incentive recommendations determine which specific economic benefit (e.g., coupons and credits) we recommend to you.

In addition to considering input signals from user interactions and contextual information listed in Section 6.2, Temu’s promotion recommendations also consider the following input signals related to promotion attributes:

(1) for promotional activity recommendations: basic activity features (e.g., promotional materials such as images and text, and activity benefits such as coupon amounts) and historical performance metrics (e.g., aggregated click-through rates which indicate how often an activity has been clicked by our users and aggregated conversion rates which indicate how likely a user who participated in an activity actually made a purchase); and

(2) for incentive recommendations: incentive features (such as coupon amounts, and the monetary amount and quantity of products you need to purchase to qualify for the incentives), historical trends of user usage of the incentive (such as usage rate), and product attributes such as product category, price, and inventory.

6.6 Non-Personalised Review Recommendations.

Temu’s review recommendations are non-personalised, meaning they do not consider input signals from user interactions with the Services. Instead, review recommendations consider review-specific attributes, such as the representativeness and informativeness of the review texts, whether the review contains images or videos, the number of images included in the review, and the clarity and relevance of the images contained in the review. Review recommendations also consider your country/region settings to prioritise reviews relevant to your country/region.

You can filter the reviews by:

(1) “rating filters” (featuring reviews with specific ratings);

(2) “photos/videos” (featuring reviews with photos or videos);

(3) “bought again” (featuring reviews from repeat purchasers); and

(4) country/region tag featuring consumer reviews from the country/region on which you are currently browsing Temu, as well as filtering tags generated based on the review content (such as “long battery life” for a wireless headphone listing).

You can also sort the reviews by:

(1) most recent: reviews shown from the newest to the oldest;

(2) highest rating: reviews with the highest ratings appear first; and

(3) lowest rating: reviews with the lowest ratings appear first.
