英国二手艺术品交易平台的反洗钱义务认定

平台是否构成艺术品市场参与者、门槛计价与注册义务

一　需要判断的法律问题

英国站以 marketplace 模式销售二手画作，平台代收货款、按成交收取 8%–11% 服务费，但不取得作品所有权；商家协议第 26.6 条又明确双方不存在合伙或代理关系。据此需要回答三个问题：

第一，平台是否属于《2017 年反洗钱条例》所称艺术品市场参与者（art market participant，下称 AMP），从而负有反洗钱义务；

第二，客户尽职调查的 £10,000 门槛以哪个价格为基数——商家设定的 Base Price，还是买家实际支付的 Retail Price；

第三，注册义务是否以存在已达门槛的交易为前提。

二　结论

**（一）平台构成 AMP。**条例第 14(1)(d)(i) 条的构成要件有二：以经营方式、担任艺术品买卖的中介。条文不以取得所有权、也不以成为合同一方为要件。平台介入定价、代收货款、处理退款与争议，属以经营方式担任中介。协议第 26.6 条只解决民事责任分配，1不改变监管上的主体识别。[1]

**（二）2门槛以 Retail Price 为基数。**第 27(7C)(a) 条以交易价值（a single operation or several operations which appear to be linked）为准，服务费与增值税一并计入。以 10% 费率计，Retail 为 Base 的 1.32 倍，Base 达 £7,576 即触及 £10,000。[2]

（三）注册不以达门槛交易为前提。🔴第 56(6) 条3要求🟠未列入登记册、且申请尚未获批者不得经营。换言之，注册是展业的前置条件，4与是否已有达门槛交易无关。[8]

**（四）此前“未达门槛”的结论不成立。**该结论以合计标价为基数，未区分 Base 与 Retail 两个口径。须以订单财务明细重算；重算完成前，建议不开放达门槛交易。

三　业务背景

平台以 marketplace 模式在英国本土销售二手画作，入驻商家自设 Base Price。平台加收 8%–11% 服务费，再按 20% 计征增值税，得出买家看到的 Retail Price：Base £100、费率 10% 时 Retail 为 £132。

货款由平台代收后与商家结算，平台不取得作品所有权；试点首批 3–5 幅，境内履约、境内收付。

四　平台是否构成 AMP

（一）条文

第 14(1)(d)(i) 条的原文是：

“art market participant” means, subject to paragraph (3), a firm or sole practitioner who— (i) by way of business trades in, or acts as an intermediary in the sale or purchase of, works of art and the value of the transaction, or a series of linked transactions, amounts to £10,000 or more.

译：“艺术品市场参与者”指，除第 (3) 款另有规定外，符合下列情形的商号或个体执业者：(i) 以经营方式买卖艺术品，或🟠担任艺术品买卖的中介，且交易金额（或关联交易金额）达 10,000 英镑或以上。

条文对中介未设形式要件，也不要求中介取得作品所有权或成为买卖合同当事人；判断落在是否以经营方式介入他人之间的买卖。第 14(3) 款排除的是艺术家出售本人作品的情形，与本模式无关。🟠平台设定费率、代收货款、处理退款与争议，🔴介入程度明显高于单纯提供信息。

（二）HMRC 的口径

HMRC ECSH54525《经济犯罪监督手册》以反向列举界定中介，明确把两类人排除在外：[12]

“a person or business that just provides contact information for a prospective buyer or seller, without 🟠getting involved in the sale”，以及“art advisors that do not receive a fee”。

手册强调判断标准是实际参与程度，而非合同如何描述双方关系。平台既介入交易，又按成交收取🟠服务费，两项排除条件均不满足。

《经济犯罪监督手册》中关于“中介”的表述：

Intermediaries 

An intermediary is someone who, 🟠by way of business, actively transacts in the sale or purchase of works of art 🟠on behalf of a seller or buyer under whose authority they act. “Actively transacts” means where an individual or business facilitates the sale or purchase of a work of art for a fee. 

An intermediary could be an art gallery, a dealer or an online sales platform, or anyone else paid by the seller or buyer for whom they act. 

Online sales platforms will be an intermediary if they 🟠receive a commission, 🟠fee, or a percentage of a sale value.5 This could include online sales platforms that are based outside of the UK but act on behalf of UK customers.

Calculating the value of transactions 

The value of a work of art is calculated as the final invoiced price for the work of art including taxes, commission, and any ancillary costs (such as delivery charges on the same invoice).

（三）26.6 条与 3.1 条的关系

UK本本B端商家协议中26.6 条约定双方不存在合伙或代理关系，作用范围限于民事责任分配；3.1 条则确认平台收取 Service Fees，费用依平台记录的交易数据计算。两者并存的结果是：🟠平台在民事上不是商家的代理人，在反洗钱监管上仍是收取报酬的中介。以 26.6 条对外说明时，宜同时披露收费安排。

（四）与欧盟规则的差异

欧盟 (EU) 2024/1624 第 3(3)(e)、(i) 条将艺术品交易商与中介分别列举，自营转售另按所有权是否转移判断。英国条例不作此区分，只看是否以经营方式介入买卖。同一模式在两个法域可能得出相反结论，本报告仅适用英国。[15]

第 3(3)(i)persons trading or acting as intermediaries in the trade of cultural goods, including when this is carried out by art galleries and auction houses, where the value of the transaction or linked transactions amounts to at least EUR 10 000 or the equivalent in national currency;

五　门槛的计价口径

（一）条文与取数

第 27(7C)(a) 条的原文是：

an art market participant must also apply customer due diligence measures— (a) in relation to any trade in a work of art (within the meaning given in regulation 14), when the firm or sole practitioner carries out, or acts in respect of, any such occasional transaction, whether executed in a single operation or in several operations which appear to be linked, whose value amounts to £10,000 or more.

译：艺术品市场参与者还须对下列情形采取客户尽职调查措施：(a) 就其经办或参与的任何艺术品交易（含义见第 14 条），无论以单次操作还是若干看似关联的操作完成，凡交易价值达 10,000 英镑或以上的临时交易。

条文以“交易价值”为基数，未限定按哪一方报价计算。买家实际支付的对价包含服务费与增值税，故取 Retail Price。2026 年第 621 号令将原欧元表述按 1:1 改为英镑，并补入 occasional 与关联交易的表述。[11]

（二）换算与临界值

Retail = Base ×（1 + 服务费率）× 1.20

三档费率下：8% 对应系数 1.296、临界 Base £7,716；10% 对应 1.320、£7,576；11% 对应 1.332、£7,508。三档相差不足 £210，内控阈值按最高档设定即可覆盖。

（三）对试点参数的影响

原按 Base 设定的 £8,000 预警、£9,000 日累计，折算为 Retail 分别是 £10,560 与 £11,880，均已越过门槛——原参数在监控上等于失效。建议改为按 Retail 设定：单笔 £8,000、累计 £9,000，并🟠对同一买家 24 小时内的多笔交易作关联合并。

七　执法现状

2025 年 4 月至 9 月，HMRC 对 35 家艺术品市场参与者（AMP）公布处罚，[https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/businesses-not-complying-with-money-laundering-regulations/businesses-that-have-not-complied-with-the-money-laundering-regulations-2025-to-2026]其中 33 笔事由为未按期申请注册（reg 56），2 笔为未履行变更/人员申报义务（reg 21、21(4)）。单笔金额介于 £1,350 至 £36,400，中位数约 £2,900，逾六成低于 £5,000。

35 家均为线下经营的艺术交易主体（画廊、经销商、拍卖行、艺术顾问、室内设计公司等），没有任何一家是第三方 marketplace（居间撮合型电商平台）。

不能作为免责依据。清单只反映“已被处罚”的样本，而 35 笔中 33 笔是 reg 56（未按期申请注册）——这是“该注册却没注册”的处罚，与主体是线上还是线下无关。平台若构成 AMP 而未注册，同样落入 reg 56

处罚重心不在交易环节的技术瑕疵，而在主体资格与制度文件是否齐备——注册是成本最低、优先级最高的一项。[13]

HMRC Guidance:

Who should register

🟠You have to register with HMRC if your business operates as an art market participant under the money laundering regulations.

An art market participant under the money laundering regulations is a company or sole practitioner who is either:

  • 🟠‘by way of business’ trading in, or acts as an intermediary in, the buying or selling of works of art where the transaction value (or a series of linked transactions) is 10,000 euros or more
  • the operator of a Freeport storing works of art in the Freeport and the value for a person (or a series of linked persons) is 10,000 euros or more

If you have questions about ‘by way of business’, contact HMRC.

Intermediaries

An intermediary is someone who, by way of business, is 🟠actively involved in the sale or purchase of a work of art, 🟠on behalf of a seller or buyer. This involves 🟠dealing with more than one party. The intermediary will have 🟠authority to act on behalf of a seller or buyer.

You should register as an art market participant if you:

  • negotiate the price of the art piece with another art market participant
  • act on behalf of a customer during a sale or purchase

You do not need to register as an art market participant if you only:

  • provide art advice to your customer
  • charge a fee to carry out research or provenance checks on a work of art but are not involved in the sale or purchase
  • 🟠introduce your customers to an art market participant who then carries out the transaction
  • provide non-regulated services, such as framing, shipping or art installation

When to register

You must register with HMRC if you 🟠meet the criteria. You’ll be able to pay your registration fees at the same time.

备注：中介的实质要件：①actively involved（积极参与）②on behalf of a seller or buyer（为一方行事）③dealing with more than one party（与不止一方打交道）④authority to act（有代理权限） “我们只是技术服务商”在合同上成立，但事实上的权限更被看重
