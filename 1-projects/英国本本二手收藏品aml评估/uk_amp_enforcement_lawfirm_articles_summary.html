英国艺术品市场（AMP）反洗钱执法：顶尖律所文章综述

研究问题：英国自 2020 年将艺术品市场纳入反洗钱监管（Art Market Participants，下称 AMP）后，是否仍处于监管初期？现有执法是否主要停留在”未按期注册”层面？英国当地律所是否有文章对该现象作出解读？

研究范围：英国本地执业律所（含国际所伦敦办公室）公开发表的 insight / article / briefing，时间跨度 2020—2026 年。

核验标准：全部链接经实际抓取，逐篇比对「标题 / 发布日期 / 所属律所 / 核心数字」四项一致性；引用保留英文原文。

一、核心结论

“英国 AMP 监管仍处早期、执法主要停留在未注册层面”这一判断有实证支持，但它描述的是”过去五年的现象”，而非”当前监管的定性”。

2025/26 年已出现六位数实质合规罚单与首例刑事定罪，执法重心正在快速迁移。因此该论据可用于支撑”历史执法强度有限”，但不宜用于推导”未来对高价值交易不会实质执法”。

维度判断证据强度
罚单数量结构以”未注册”为主✅ 成立强（官方数据 + 律所解读）
处罚对象以小微画廊 / 独立经销商为主✅ 成立强（官方清单可逐笔核对）
监管规则本身尚在成型期✅ 成立中（2026 年仍有修订）
实质合规执法”尚未开始”❌ 已不成立强（£158,679 罚单、刑事定罪）

二、支撑现有判断的证据

2.1 罚单结构确实”几乎全部为未注册”

Stephenson Harwood（2026 年 3 月 3 日）对 HMRC 于 2026 年 2 月 9 日公布数据的解读：

“HMRC issued 335 penalty notices in the period 1 April 2025 to 30 September 2025 for breaches of the MLRs. Art market participants (‘AMPs’) were the third most fined sector in this period, behind estate agency businesses and accountancy service providers. 35 fines were published against AMPs in total, with all but one of these fines being for a breach of regulation 56 (failure to register at the required time). The sole fine for AMPs that was not for a breach of regulation 56 was a £5,000 fine for a breach of regulation 21 (failure to notify HMRC of a material change). The largest financial penalty for an AMP in this period was £36,400.”

同一篇文章中，作者对”实质合规罚单仍然很少”的表述：

“Published fines for substantive AML failings (beyond registration issues) have so far been few in the art market. However, the trends in HMRC’s AML supervisory and enforcement activity generally and the apparent gap in compliance HMRC is finding across the sectors it regulates (it rates a majority of firms (60%) as ‘non-compliant’) suggest that tougher action and sanctions are coming.”

⚠️ 注意：该段前半句可用于支撑判断，但后半句是明确的反向预警，引用时须完整保留，否则易被反驳。

Mishcon de Reya（2022 年 8 月 26 日）更早提出同一”先易后难”逻辑：

“Although HMRC may initially be going after the low hanging fruit of those who have failed to register, it seems that some who have registered in time are already receiving letters from HMRC giving notice of intended audits.”

2.3 “监管年轻”的事实基础

DLA Piper（2021 年 6 月 6 日）：

“Art market participants have been subject to the other requirements of the MLRs 2017 since 10 January 2020… from 10 June 2021, trading whilst not registered is a criminal offence and may result in a civil action or criminal prosecution.”

WilmerHale（2023 年 4 月 19 日）引用 FATF 报告，指出行业结构性薄弱：

“FATF notes that reporting of suspicious transactions by AMPs is low, even in jurisdictions like the UK where AMPs are required to undertake due diligence on their customers and report concerns.”

Kingsley Napley（2021 年新闻稿）指出受影响主体的规模特征：

“The vast majority of those affected by the new regulations are sole traders or micro-enterprises that simply do not have the financial resources or staff capacity to invest in meeting these regulatory requirements.”

三、反向证据：执法正在升级

以下三项若不同时呈现，论证将在质询中被直接推翻。建议在对外材料中主动披露。

3.1 已出现六位数实质合规罚单

Withers（2026 年 6 月 29 日）：

“Looking beyond this period, it is apparent that HMRC’s focus has evolved. There is now increasing scrutiny of the effectiveness of AMPs’ AML policies and frameworks, and the manner in which these policies and procedures are implemented on a day-to-day basis. This is illustrated by the penalty imposed on DYS44 Art Gallery Ltd, which was fined £158,679 for extensive procedural failures, including deficiencies in its risk assessments, policies, staff training and due diligence processes (during the period between 1 October 2024 and 31 March 2025).“

3.2 已出现司法确认与刑事定罪

“in Kanbi Contemporary Ltd v HMRC [2026] UKFTT 841 (TC), the First Tier Tribunal upheld HMRC’s penalty levied on the gallery for operating as an unregistered AMP despite the fact that it was a small art business and the tribunal acknowledged that it took ‘significant steps’ to meet its regulatory obligations.”

“in June 2025, a UK art dealer, Oghenochuko Ojiri, was convicted under section 21a of the Terrorism Act 2000 – the first prosecution under that provision – which makes it a criminal offence to fail to disclose knowledge or suspicion of terrorist financing in the regulated sector.”

— Withers，2026 年 6 月 29 日

3.3 风险评级虽降级，但仍被列为最高固有风险行业

NRA 2025 将艺术品市场从 “high” 下调为 “medium risk”，理由为：

“HMRC’s improved understanding of the risk in the sector, and therefore our ability to respond to it has driven the decrease in the score. However, there remains a risk of the sector being used for money laundering.”

但同期 AML supervision report（2025 年 12 月）写明：

“HMRC identified trust and company service providers, money service businesses and art market participants as the sectors presenting the highest inherent risks for money laundering.”

— 转引自 Stephenson Harwood 与 Withers

3.4 合规率数据同样不利

HMRC 2024/25 评估结果（Stephenson Harwood 引述）：60% 为 non-compliant，仅 19% 为 fully compliant，21% 为 generally compliant。HMRC 期间共完成 451 次案头审查、792 次现场走访，非合规最常见形态为：政策与程序文件不足、未能按要求提供资料、客户风险评估不足、记录保存不足。

3.5 规则本身仍在收紧

Withers 指出 MLR 修订于 2026 年 6 月 30 日 生效，与 AMP 相关的变化包括：

  • 门槛由 €10,000 改为 £10,000（按欧元对英镑近似等值）；
  • EDD 触发条件由 “complex or unusually large” 收窄为 “unusually complex or unusually large”；
  • FATF 灰名单不再自动触发 EDD，仅”行动呼吁”名单（朝鲜、伊朗、缅甸）自动适用；
  • 另据同一文章，自 2025 年 5 月 14 日起，AMP 与 HVD 已被纳入英国金融制裁报告义务主体，须向 OFSI 报告；民事执法按 strict liability 认定，合规体系缺失属加重情节。

四、对业务决策的实际含义

4.1 可用的论点

  • 2021—2024 年 AMP 执法确实以”注册合规”为绝对主体；
  • 实质 CDD / 风险评估类罚单在 2025 年前几乎为零，DYS44（£158,679）、Unit London（£20,000）属近期个例；
  • 因此”针对高价值艺术品交易的穿透式、实质合规执法案例尚不丰富”这一表述成立。

4.2 不可用的论点

  • 不可表述为”英国监管还在初步阶段，因此风险低”——HMRC 明确将 AMP 列为三大最高固有风险行业，已出现六位数罚单与刑事定罪，且司法裁判（Kanbi）明确表明企业规模小不构成免责。

4.3 限额管控的正确定位

建议将限额管控定位为内部操作层面的风险收敛措施（de-risking by design），而非”因监管不成熟而可以减少动作”。

文件记录上建议同时体现两点：

  1. 交易结构已将单笔及关联交易金额控制在阈值以下；
  2. 即便如此仍保留基础 CDD / KYC 与制裁筛查——因制裁义务不受金额阈值限制（OFSI 按 strict liability 认定），且 NCA 的 SAR 义务以”怀疑”而非”金额”为触发标准。
